Privacy Policy for Draxie Store

Last updated: January 07, 2026

Welcome to Draxie Store – your destination for elegant fashion! This Privacy Policy explains how Draxie Store ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and protects your personal information when you shop with us through our online store at draxiestore.github.io. Your privacy matters to us, and we're committed to keeping your information safe.

By using Draxie Store, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

Personal Information

When you shop with us or create an account, we collect:

  • Contact Information: Full name, email address, and phone number
  • Shipping Details: Delivery address for sending your fashion items (skirts, blazers, dresses, tops, pants, bikinis, bags, and shoes)
  • Billing Information: Billing address for payment processing
  • Payment Information: Payment card details, mobile money information, or other payment methods (processed securely through our payment partners)
  • Account Information: Username, password, and shopping preferences

Shopping and Browsing Data

We automatically collect information about your shopping experience:

  • Products viewed and added to cart (dresses, tops, shoes, bags, etc.)
  • Search queries and browsing history on our site
  • Purchase history and order details
  • Size and style preferences
  • Device information (IP address, browser type, operating system)
  • Pages visited and time spent on our website

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies to enhance your shopping experience:

  • Essential Cookies: Keep you logged in, remember your cart items, and process your orders
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your size preferences, favorite styles, and language settings
  • Analytics Cookies: Help us understand which fashion items are popular and how customers navigate our store
  • Marketing Cookies: Show you relevant fashion recommendations and special offers

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to provide the best shopping experience:

  • Process Orders: Complete your purchases of clothing, shoes, bags, and accessories
  • Payment Processing: Securely handle online payments through our trusted payment partners
  • Delivery: Ship your fashion items to your specified address
  • Account Management: Maintain your profile, order history, and saved preferences
  • Customer Support: Respond to your questions about products, orders, sizing, or returns
  • Personalization: Recommend styles, outfits, and products based on your browsing and purchase history
  • Marketing: Send you updates about new arrivals, seasonal collections, sales, and special promotions (you can opt out anytime)
  • Fraud Prevention: Protect your account and detect suspicious payment activity
  • Service Improvement: Analyze shopping trends to expand our product range and improve our website

3. Payment Information Security

Your payment security is our priority. We partner with trusted payment processors to handle all transactions securely. We do not store complete credit card numbers or sensitive payment information on our servers.

Payment methods we support include:

  • Credit and debit cards (Visa, Mastercard)
  • Mobile money services (MTN, Airtel)
  • Bank transfers
  • Other secure payment gateways

All payment transactions are encrypted using industry-standard SSL technology. However, while we implement strong security measures, no online transaction can be guaranteed 100% secure.

4. Sharing Your Information

We may share your information with trusted partners to fulfill our services:

  • Payment Processors: To securely process your online payments
  • Shipping Companies: To deliver your fashion items to your doorstep
  • Service Providers: For website hosting, email services, and customer support
  • Marketing Partners: Only if you've opted in to receive promotional content (we never sell your data)
  • Legal Authorities: When required by law or to protect our rights and prevent fraud

We ensure all partners maintain strict confidentiality and security standards.

5. Data Retention

We keep your information only as long as necessary:

  • Active Accounts: Data retained while your account is active
  • Purchase Records: Stored for 7 years for tax and legal compliance
  • Marketing Data: Until you unsubscribe or request deletion
  • Analytics Data: Anonymized after 2 years

6. Your Rights and Choices

You have control over your personal information:

  • Access: Request a copy of your personal data
  • Correction: Update your account information, shipping address, or preferences
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your account and personal data (subject to legal requirements)
  • Opt-Out: Unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time
  • Cookie Control: Manage cookie preferences through your browser settings

7. Data Security

We implement multiple security measures to protect your information:

  • SSL encryption for all data transmission
  • Secure servers with restricted access
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • Employee training on data protection
  • Two-factor authentication for accounts

Despite our efforts, no system is completely secure. Please protect your password and notify us immediately if you suspect unauthorized access.

8. Children's Privacy

Draxie Store is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13 years old. If you're under 18, please have a parent or guardian make purchases on your behalf. If we discover we've collected data from a child under 13, we'll delete it immediately.

9. International Customers

Draxie Store operates from Uganda. If you're shopping from another country, your information will be transferred to and processed in Uganda. By using our service, you consent to this transfer.

10. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to social media, payment partners, or other websites. We're not responsible for their privacy practices. Please review their policies before sharing information.

11. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.
